# Age Assurance Objective

The age-assurance objective is trained on the existing probabilistic age-regression output, not on a separate classifier head. For each image, the age CNN predicts a Gaussian age distribution with mean `mu` and variance `sigma^2`; the adult probability is the Gaussian tail probability `P(age >= 18)`, computed as `1 - CDF((18 - mu) / sigma)`. The assurance loss has two parts: a BCE term between this adult probability and the binary adult label, and a unified severity term that multiplies the per-sample BCE by the same symmetric boundary-distance weight on both sides of the threshold: `min(1, abs(age - threshold) / radius)`. This makes the extra penalty increase linearly as samples move farther away from the legal boundary, saturating at the configured radius, so severe mistakes for clearly minor or clearly adult samples are penalized more strongly while still sharing the calibrated Gaussian regression head used for age prediction.
