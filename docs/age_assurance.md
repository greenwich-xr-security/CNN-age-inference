# Age Assurance Objective

The age-assurance objective is trained on the existing probabilistic age-regression output, not on a separate classifier head. For each image, the age CNN predicts a Gaussian age distribution with mean `mu` and variance `sigma^2`; the adult probability is the Gaussian tail probability `P(age >= 18)`, computed as `1 - CDF((18 - mu) / sigma)`. The assurance loss has three parts: a BCE term between this adult probability and the binary adult label, a minor-admission severity term for minors whose predicted adult probability is too high, and an adult-rejection severity term for adults whose predicted adult probability is too low. The two severity terms are distance-weighted by age relative to the observed training age range: minors farther below the 18-year boundary receive higher false-accept severity, while adults farther above the boundary receive higher false-reject severity. This keeps the assurance objective aligned with the security risk of accepting underage users and the usability risk of rejecting adults, while still sharing the calibrated Gaussian regression head used for age prediction.
